# Visual verification — admin operations and separated reward links

The workspace preview now shows three independent mission cards with separate Open link buttons: Link4Sub level 1 (+25), Link4Sub level 2 (+50), and Link4M (+25). The previous shared Choose link modal and auto-claim path are removed. It includes a recent coin claim table and a Fraud watch panel that surfaces burst attempts and repeated incomplete attempts for review instead of automatically banning accounts.
